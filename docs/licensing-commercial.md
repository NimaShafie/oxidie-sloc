oxide-sloc is published under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](../LICENSE). The AGPL requires that any
modified or extended version distributed as a network service also be released under the same
license. For organisations that cannot accept this requirement, a commercial license is available.

## When a commercial license applies

| Scenario | AGPL sufficient? | Commercial license needed? |
|----------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Internal use — running oxide-sloc for your own team's analysis | Yes | No |
| Distributing oxide-sloc as part of an open-source product | Yes (if AGPL-compatible) | No |
| Embedding oxide-sloc in a SaaS product served to third parties | No | Yes |
| Shipping oxide-sloc inside a proprietary, closed-source product | No | Yes |
| Proprietary add-ons or forks you do not intend to open-source | No | Yes |
| Consulting or custom integrations delivered to a client under NDA | Discuss | Contact us |

---

## What a commercial license covers

- **Proprietary distribution rights** — embed or bundle oxide-sloc without AGPL obligations
- **Enterprise features** — access to features not included in the AGPL release
- **Support agreements** — guaranteed response SLAs, direct engineering contact
- **Custom integrations** — bespoke connectors, report formats, or workflow automation
- **Dual-licensed distribution** — distribute oxide-sloc to your customers under a separate license
